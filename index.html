Research

From X-rays to
gamma rays.

My research connects the long-term variability of the Crab system with the development of analysis methods for MeV pulsars.

Working with Prof. Hsiang-Kuang Chang at NTHU, I investigate how flux, spectral shape, and pulse phase can constrain high-energy emission from neutron stars and their surrounding nebulae. My work combines archival observations from Swift/BAT, RXTE, NuSTAR, and Fermi/LAT with simulated COSI data.

A central aim is to distinguish the variable emission of the Crab Nebula from that of the pulsar, while developing timing and phase-resolved analysis tools for the MeV band.

Projects

Three connected
research directions.

01 / NEBULAPublished research

Crab Nebula spectral anticorrelation

Investigating the relationship between X-ray flux and photon index in the Crab system. Our Swift/BAT study found that brighter emission is associated with harder spectra, with the nebula dominating the signal. A complementary RXTE and NuSTAR study examined this behaviour across soft and hard X-ray bands.

This work explores how spectral variability constrains synchrotron emission in the pulsar wind nebula.

Swift/BAT · RXTE/PCA & HEXTE · NuSTAR · X-ray spectroscopy

Swift/BAT paper ↗RXTE & NuSTAR paper ↗
02 / PULSAROngoing research

Long-term variability of the Crab pulsar

Studying the evolution of the Crab pulsar's gamma-ray flux and spectral shape using Fermi/LAT observations. The analysis combines time-resolved likelihood fitting with rotational phase selection to investigate the pulsar separately from the surrounding nebula.

Comparisons with phase-resolved X-ray observations examine how variability changes across energy bands and whether spectral and flux changes are related.

Fermi/LAT · Pulsar timing · Phase selection · Time-resolved spectroscopy

03 / METHODSIn development

Phase-resolved analysis tools for COSI

Developing a pulsar analysis workflow for simulated Compton Spectrometer and Imager (COSI) observations, using the Crab as a test case. The work brings together event folding, pulse profiles, phaseograms, and periodicity statistics.

Energy-resolved profiles and phase selection support the development of phase-resolved spectroscopy methods for studying pulsar emission in the MeV band.

COSI simulations · Python · MEGAlib · Z² statistics & H-test
